UčestvujContribute

Pilot je napravljen da ga nastavnik i student mogu menjati bez dozvole, bez alata za građenje i bez naloga igde osim na GitHubu. The pilot is built so a teacher or a student can change it without permission, without a build tool, and without an account anywhere except GitHub.

Prijavi grešku ili nejasnoćuReport an error or a confusion

Otvori issue i napiši šta si uneo, šta je alat pokazao i šta si očekivao. Nejasna formulacija zadatka je ravnopravna greška sa pogrešnim brojem: ako si ti pogrešno razumeo, pogrešiće i sledeća godina. Open an issue with what you entered, what the tool showed and what you expected. An unclear task statement counts as much as a wrong number: if you misread it, so will next year's class.

Dodaj tip zadatkaAdd a task type

Jedna funkcija u assets/zadaci.js: vrati tekst na oba jezika, jedinicu, tačan odgovor izračunat pozivom jezgra i korake. Nemoj pisati sopstvenu formulu za tačan odgovor — tako zadatak i jezgro ne mogu da se raziđu. One function in assets/zadaci.js: return the text in both languages, the unit, the correct answer computed by calling the core, and the steps. Do not write your own formula for the answer — that is what keeps the task and the core in sync.

nastavnikteacher

Prilagodi programAdapt the programme

Nedelje su u program.html, u običnim <details> blokovima. Promeni raspored poena, zameni nedelju, prepiši cilj — nema šablona koji to zabranjuje. Ako tvoj akreditovani silabus traži drugi redosled, pilot se savija, a ne silabus. The weeks live in program.html as plain <details> blocks. Change the points, replace a week, rewrite a goal — no template forbids it. If your accredited syllabus needs another order, the pilot bends, not the syllabus.

Kako se radi lokalnoWorking locally

Nema koraka građenja, nema npm install, nema zavisnosti. Kloniraj i otvori index.html: No build step, no npm install, no dependencies. Clone and open index.html:

git clone https://github.com/3esign/mechanics-lab
cd mechanics-lab
node tests/mehanika.test.js     # 77 provera / 77 checks
node tests/sajt.test.js         # veze, id-jevi, bez spoljnih resursa

Pravilo pre spajanja: nov proračun dolazi sa testom. Ako dodaješ trapezno opterećenje, dodaj i poznato rešenje za njega, i pusti da ga provere invarijante iz tačke 8 u testu. Ako testovi padnu, kod je kriv, ne test. Rule before merging: a new calculation arrives with a test. If you add trapezoidal loading, add its closed-form check too and let the invariants in test section 8 cover it. If the tests fail, the code is wrong, not the test.

Šta se neće prihvatitiWhat will not be accepted

  • Zavisnost iz npm, CDN skripta, font sa interneta, analitika, kolačić. Sajt mora raditi iz foldera, bez mreže i bez praćenja. An npm dependency, a CDN script, a remote font, analytics, a cookie. The site must run from a folder, offline and untracked.
  • Proračun bez testa, ili test prilagođen da prođe kraj pogrešnog koda. A calculation without a test, or a test bent to pass broken code.
  • Rezultat koji ne kaže svoju granicu. Ako nova provera pretpostavlja klasu preseka, bočnu stabilnost ili vrednost iz nacionalnog priloga, to mora da se ispiše uz broj. A result that hides its limit. If a new check assumes a section class, lateral stability or a national-annex value, that must be printed next to the number.
  • Tvrdnja da je alat za projektovanje. Nije i ne treba da postane: za to postoje overeni programi i ovlašćeni inženjer. A claim that this is design software. It is not and should not become one: that is what certified programs and licensed engineers are for.

Sledeći koraci pilotaNext steps for the pilot

  1. Uporediti program sa zvaničnim akreditovanim silabusom predmeta i pomeriti nedelje gde treba. Compare the programme with the official accredited syllabus and move weeks where needed.
  2. Rešetke u laboratoriji: metoda čvorova sa prikazom pritisnutih i zategnutih štapova (nedelja 6 je sada bez instrumenta). Trusses in the lab: method of joints with compression and tension members shown (week 6 currently has no instrument).
  3. Smicanje po EN 1992 sa uzengijama i tablični Wpl za valjane profile. EN 1992 shear with stirrups, and tabulated Wpl for rolled profiles.
  4. Probni čas sa stvarnom grupom i jedno merenje: koliko studenata tačno predvidi pomeranje vrha momenta pre nego što vidi dijagram. A trial lecture with a real group and one measurement: how many students correctly predict the shift of the moment peak before seeing the diagram.
