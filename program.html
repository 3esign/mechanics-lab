Program: petnaest nedeljaProgramme: fifteen weeks

Luk je jedan: od sile do dimenzionisane grede. Prvih šest nedelja je statika, sledećih pet otpornost materijala, poslednje četiri su beton, drvo i čelik po Evrokodu. Petnaesta nedelja uzima jednu istu gredu i dimenzioniše je u sva tri materijala, da student vidi koliko materijal menja presek, a koliko ne menja mehaniku. One arc: from a force to a designed beam. Six weeks of statics, five of strength of materials, four of concrete, timber and steel to the Eurocodes. Week fifteen takes a single beam and designs it in all three materials, so students see how much the material changes the section and how little it changes the mechanics.

Ishodi učenjaLearning outcomes

Posle petnaest nedelja student ume da:After fifteen weeks a student can:

  1. nacrta tačan dijagram oslobođenog tela i prepozna sopstvenu grešku u njemu;draw a correct free-body diagram and recognise their own error in it;
  2. odredi reakcije i nacrta dijagrame N, V i M za statički određen nosač;find reactions and draw N, V and M diagrams for a determinate member;
  3. izračuna geometrijske karakteristike preseka i objasni zašto visina ulazi na kvadrat;compute section properties and explain why depth enters squared;
  4. poveže moment sa naponom i ugibom i proceni red veličine pre računa;link moment to stress and deflection and estimate the order of magnitude before calculating;
  5. dimenzioniše jednostavnu gredu u betonu, drvu i čeliku po Evrokodu i napiše šta je pretpostavio;design a simple beam in concrete, timber and steel to the Eurocodes and state the assumptions made;
  6. kaže gde njegov proračun prestaje da važi.say where their own calculation stops being valid.

Šesti ishod se ocenjuje. Zadatak u kome je student napisao granicu svog modela nosi više poena od zadatka sa tačnim brojem bez granice — jer inženjerska greška u praksi gotovo nikad nije aritmetička. The sixth outcome is graded. A solution that states the limits of its model scores higher than a correct number with no stated limits — because engineering failures in practice are almost never arithmetic.

NedeljeWeeks

01Sila, moment, ravnotežaForce, moment, equilibrium

Cilj: sila kao vektor, moment kao sila puta krak, tri jednačine ravnoteže u ravni. Znak se bira jednom i ne menja se do kraja semestra. Goal: force as a vector, moment as force times lever arm, three planar equilibrium equations. The sign convention is chosen once and never changed again.

Vežba: ravnoteža na stvarnim predmetima u sali — vrata na šarkama, polica, stolica. Izmeri krak metrom pre nego što napišeš jednačinu. Tutorial: equilibrium on real objects in the room — a door on hinges, a shelf, a chair. Measure the lever arm with a tape before writing the equation.

Laboratorija: instrument 1, pomeri silu i gledaj reakcije.Lab: instrument 1, move the load and watch the reactions.

02Oslonci i statička određenostSupports and determinacy

Cilj: pokretni oslonac, nepokretni oslonac, ukleštenje; broj nepoznatih prema broju jednačina. Kad je sistem statički neodređen, student to prepoznaje u prvom redu rešenja, a ne na kraju. Goal: roller, pin, fixed end; unknowns versus equations. When a system is indeterminate, the student recognises it in the first line of the solution, not at the end.

Vežba: isti nosač, četiri rasporeda oslonaca. Dva su rešiva ručno, jedan je labilan, jedan je neodređen — razvrstaj ih prije računa. Tutorial: one member, four support layouts. Two are solvable by hand, one is a mechanism, one is indeterminate — classify them before calculating.

Laboratorija: dodaj treći oslonac i pročitaj poruku rešavača. Alat kaže „ne umem", ne izmišlja broj.Lab: add a third support and read the solver message. The tool says "I cannot", it does not invent a number.

03Dijagram oslobođenog telaThe free-body diagram

Cilj: izolovati telo, zameniti svaki oslonac silom, ne nacrtati ni jednu silu koju telo samo proizvodi. Ova nedelja je najvažnija u semestru: istraživanja u nastavi mehanike dosledno nalaze da greška u dijagramu oslobođenog tela nosi greške kroz ceo kurs. Goal: isolate the body, replace every support with a force, draw no force the body exerts on itself. This is the most important week of the term: engineering-education research consistently finds that FBD errors propagate through the whole course.

Vežba: nastavnik deli četiri dijagrama od kojih tri imaju po jednu grešku; studenti ih nalaze i imenuju. Mnemonik: telo — reakcije — spoljne sile — osa — dimenzije. Tutorial: four diagrams handed out, three with one error each; students find and name them. Mnemonic: body — reactions — external forces — axes — dimensions.

04Dijagrami V i M: prosta gredaV and M diagrams: simply supported beam

Cilj: V kao zbir sila levo od reza, M kao zbir momenata levo od reza. Veza dV/dx = −q i dM/dx = V, i šta ona znači na slici: tamo gde je V = 0, moment ima vrh. Goal: V as the sum of forces left of the cut, M as the sum of moments left of the cut. The relations dV/dx = −q and dM/dx = V, and what they mean on the plot: where V = 0, the moment peaks.

Vežba: qL²/8 i PL/4 se izvode, ne pamte. Student koji zna izvod ne pogreši kad opterećenje nije simetrično. Tutorial: qL²/8 and PL/4 are derived, not memorised. A student who can derive them does not fail when the load is not symmetric.

Laboratorija: povuci P duž raspona i gledaj kako vrh momenta prati nulu transverzalne sile.Lab: move P along the span and watch the moment peak follow the zero of shear.

05Konzola, prepust, skokoviCantilever, overhang, jumps

Cilj: negativan moment i zategnuto gornje vlakno; skok u V pod koncentrisanom silom i skok u M pod koncentrisanim momentom. Prepust je prvi slučaj gde dijagram menja znak unutar jednog nosača. Goal: negative moment and tension in the top fibre; the jump in V under a point load and the jump in M under an applied moment. The overhang is the first case where the diagram changes sign within one member.

Vežba: gde armatura ide gore, a gde dole. Ovo je mesto gde statika prvi put kaže nešto o građenju. Tutorial: where the reinforcement goes on top and where at the bottom. This is where statics first says something about building.

06RešetkeTrusses

Cilj: metoda čvorova i metoda preseka; prepoznavanje štapova bez sile. Umesto dva mehanička recepta, jedno pravilo: izaberi telo tako da nepoznatih bude onoliko koliko imaš jednačina. Goal: method of joints and method of sections; spotting zero-force members. Instead of two mechanical recipes, one rule: choose the body so that the unknowns match the equations available.

Vežba: krovna rešetka hale iz stvarnog projekta, pritisnuti i zategnuti štapovi obojeni različito. Tutorial: a roof truss from a real project, compression and tension members coloured differently.

07Kolokvijum 1 · statikaIn-term test 1 · statics

20 poena. Jedan nosač, jedna rešetka, jedan konceptualni zadatak („ovaj dijagram ne može biti tačan — zašto?"). Dozvoljen je jedan list sa sopstvenim beleškama; formule se ne pamte, pamti se postupak. 20 points. One beam, one truss, one conceptual item ("this diagram cannot be right — why?"). One page of your own notes allowed; formulas are not memorised, the procedure is.

08Geometrijske karakteristike presekaSection properties

Cilj: težište, moment inercije, Štajnerov dodatak, otporni moment. Zašto je Iy = bh³/12 a Wy = bh²/6 i zašto I-presek postoji. Goal: centroid, second moment of area, the parallel-axis term, section modulus. Why Iy = bh³/12 while Wy = bh²/6, and why the I-section exists.

Laboratorija: instrument 2. Prebaci isti materijal iz pravougaonika u I presek i pročitaj koliko se Wy promenio pri istoj površini.Lab: instrument 2. Move the same area from a rectangle into an I-section and read how much Wy changes.

09Napon i deformacijaStress and strain

Cilj: σ = N/A, ε = Δl/l, Hukov zakon, modul elastičnosti kao izmerena osobina materijala a ne konstanta iz tablice. Poređenje E za beton, drvo i čelik i red veličine koji iz toga sledi. Goal: σ = N/A, ε = Δl/l, Hooke's law, modulus of elasticity as a measured material property rather than a table constant. Comparing E for concrete, timber and steel and the orders of magnitude that follow.

10Savijanje i smicanjeBending and shear

Cilj: σ = M/W kao posledica linearne raspodele napona po visini; τ = 1.5V/A za pravougaonik. Zašto pukotina u betonskoj gredi ide koso blizu oslonca a vertikalno u sredini raspona. Goal: σ = M/W as a consequence of linear stress distribution over the depth; τ = 1.5V/A for a rectangle. Why a crack in a concrete beam runs diagonally near the support and vertically at midspan.

11Ugib, vitkost, izvijanjeDeflection, slenderness, buckling

Cilj: 5qL⁴/384EI i zašto raspon ulazi na četvrtu; granica upotrebljivosti L/300 i L/250; Ojlerova kritična sila i vitkost kao mera. Prvi put se javlja razlika između „nosi" i „može da se koristi". Goal: 5qL⁴/384EI and why span enters to the fourth power; serviceability limits of L/300 and L/250; Euler's critical load and slenderness. The first appearance of the difference between "carries" and "is usable".

Zadaci, nivo 3: ugib C24 grede. Isti presek prolazi po nosivosti i pada po ugibu — to je poenta zadatka.Tasks, level 3: deflection of a C24 beam. The same section passes strength and fails deflection — that is the point of the task.

12Evrokod: dejstva i granična stanjaEurocode: actions and limit states

Cilj: EN 1990 i EN 1991: karakteristična i proračunska vrednost, parcijalni koeficijenti, kombinacija 1.35G + 1.5Q, granično stanje nosivosti prema graničnom stanju upotrebljivosti. Zašto koeficijent nije broj iz vazduha nego izražena nesigurnost. Goal: EN 1990 and EN 1991: characteristic and design values, partial factors, the 1.35G + 1.5Q combination, ultimate versus serviceability limit state. Why a partial factor is not an arbitrary number but expressed uncertainty.

Vežba: prikupljanje opterećenja za jednu stvarnu međuspratnu konstrukciju, sloj po sloj, sa debljinama i specifičnim težinama. Tutorial: load take-off for one real floor, layer by layer, with thicknesses and unit weights.

13Beton: EN 1992Concrete: EN 1992

Cilj: pravougaoni blok napona, μ, ξ = x/d, krak unutrašnjih sila z, potrebna armatura As. Granica duktilnosti ξ ≤ 0.45 i šta se fizički dešava kada se prekorači. Goal: the rectangular stress block, μ, ξ = x/d, the internal lever arm z, required reinforcement As. The ductility limit ξ ≤ 0.45 and what physically happens when it is exceeded.

Laboratorija: instrument 3, beton. Povećavaj MEd dok presuda ne postane crvena, pa pročitaj razlog.Lab: instrument 3, concrete. Increase MEd until the verdict turns red, then read the reason.

14Drvo i čelik · Kolokvijum 2Timber and steel · In-term test 2

Drvo, EN 1995: kmod kao sprega klase eksploatacije i trajanja opterećenja, kh, γM = 1.3; drvo je jedini od tri materijala čija čvrstoća zavisi od toga koliko dugo opterećenje stoji. Timber, EN 1995: kmod as the coupling of service class and load duration, kh, γM = 1.3; timber is the only one of the three materials whose strength depends on how long the load stays.

Čelik, EN 1993: klase preseka, Mc,Rd = Wplfy/γM0, Vpl,Rd; razlika elastičnog i plastičnog otpornog momenta na istom profilu. Steel, EN 1993: cross-section classes, Mc,Rd = Wplfy/γM0, Vpl,Rd; elastic versus plastic section modulus on the same profile.

Kolokvijum 2, 20 poena: jedno dimenzionisanje po izboru materijala, uz obavezno navedene pretpostavke. Test 2, 20 points: one design check in a material of your choice, with assumptions explicitly stated.

15Jedna greda, tri materijalaOne beam, three materials

Cilj: ista greda, isti raspon, isto opterećenje — dimenzionisana u betonu, drvu i čeliku. Uporedi visinu preseka, sopstvenu težinu, ugib i broj pretpostavki koje si morao da napišeš. Odbrana je usmena, pet minuta, bez slajdova. Goal: the same beam, span and load — designed in concrete, timber and steel. Compare section depth, self-weight, deflection and the number of assumptions you had to write down. A five-minute oral defence, no slides.

Ovo je zadatak koji odvaja studenta koji je naučio postupak od studenta koji je naučio mehaniku. Postupak daje tri broja; mehanika objašnjava zašto su različiti. This is the task that separates a student who learned the procedure from one who learned the mechanics. The procedure yields three numbers; the mechanics explains why they differ.
