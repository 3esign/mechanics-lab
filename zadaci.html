ZadaciTasks

Brojevi su novi u svakom zadatku, a tačan odgovor računa isto jezgro koje radi u laboratoriji. Dozvoljeno odstupanje je 2%, da zaokruživanje ne bude kazna. Koraci se vide i pre i posle odgovora — ovde se uči, ne ocenjuje. Every task gets fresh numbers, and the correct answer is computed by the same core that runs the lab. Tolerance is 2%, so rounding is not punished. Steps are available before and after you answer — this is for learning, not grading.
