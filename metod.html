Metod, izvori, graniceMethod, sources, limits

Ova strana postoji da bi nastavnik mogao da proveri alat pre nego što ga unese u salu, a student da vidi na čemu stoji broj koji mu je mašina dala. This page exists so a teacher can audit the tool before bringing it into a classroom, and a student can see what the machine's number stands on.

Četiri odluke o nastaviFour teaching decisions

1. Dijagram oslobođenog tela dobija celu nedelju1. The free-body diagram gets a whole week

Literatura iz nastave inženjerstva dosledno pokazuje da se greške u mehanici koncentrišu u dijagramu oslobođenog tela, a ne u aritmetici: izostavljena sila i sila koju telo deluje na samo sebe su dve najčešće. Postoje i merni instrumenti za to — Statics Concept Inventory (2002) i Statics Skills Inventory (2003), koji posebno mere modelovanje i dijagrame. Zato je nedelja 3 cela posvećena dijagramu, a vežba je nalaženje greške u gotovom dijagramu, ne crtanje novog. Engineering-education literature consistently locates mechanics errors in the free-body diagram rather than in arithmetic: a missing force and a force the body exerts on itself are the two most common. There are instruments for it — the Statics Concept Inventory (2002) and the Statics Skills Inventory (2003), which measure modelling and diagramming separately. So week 3 is entirely about the diagram, and the tutorial is finding the error in a given diagram, not drawing a new one.

2. Predviđanje pre računa2. Predict before you calculate

Instrument u laboratoriji nije kalkulator, nego klizač. Pitanje na času je uvek isto: ako pomerim silu ovamo, šta će se desiti sa momentom — i student kaže pre nego što alat pokaže. Alat koji odmah daje tačan broj bez prethodnog pitanja pravi studenta koji ume da unese podatke, a ne studenta koji ume da proceni. The lab instrument is not a calculator, it is a slider. The question in class is always the same: if I move the load here, what happens to the moment — and the student answers before the tool shows. A tool that hands over the right number without that question produces a student who can enter data, not one who can estimate.

3. Brojevi se menjaju, zadatak ostaje3. The numbers change, the task stays

Svaki zadatak dobija nove brojeve na svako učitavanje, pa prepisivanje rešenja ne pomaže, a ponavljanje istog postupka pomaže. Tolerancija je 2% da zaokruživanje ne bude kazna. Koraci su dostupni i pre odgovora: cilj je da student nauči, ne da ga sistem uhvati. Every task gets fresh numbers on each load, so copying an answer does not help while repeating the procedure does. Tolerance is 2% so rounding is not punished. Steps are available before answering: the goal is that the student learns, not that the system catches them out.

4. Granica se ispisuje, ne prećutkuje4. The limit is printed, not hidden

Kad presek ne zadovoljava, alat ne piše samo „ne zadovoljava" nego i razlog i veličinu koja je prekoračena. Kad je neka vrednost pretpostavljena (Wpl iz Wel), stoji zvezdica. Kad sistem izlazi iz domašaja rešavača (treći oslonac), poziv baca grešku umesto da vrati broj. Šesti ishod učenja — „student ume da kaže gde njegov proračun prestaje da važi" — ne može da se nauči od alata koji to sam ne radi. When a section fails, the tool prints the reason and the quantity exceeded, not just "fails". When a value is assumed (Wpl from Wel), it carries an asterisk. When a system is outside the solver's reach (a third support), the call throws instead of returning a number. The sixth learning outcome — "the student can say where their calculation stops being valid" — cannot be learned from a tool that does not do it itself.

Kako se jezgro proveravaHow the core is tested

Jezgro (assets/mehanika.js) ima 77 provera u tests/mehanika.test.js, bez ijedne zavisnosti: The core (assets/mehanika.js) carries 77 checks in tests/mehanika.test.js, with zero dependencies:

  • Poznata rešenja: qL²/8, PL/4, Pab/L, qL²/2, 3qL²/32 i skok momenta jednak primenjenom momentu. Closed-form solutions: qL²/8, PL/4, Pab/L, qL²/2, 3qL²/32, and a moment jump equal to the applied moment.
  • Invarijante na 200 nasumičnih greda: suma sila i suma momenata su nula, i numerički izvod momenta se slaže sa transverzalnom silom (dM/dx = V) u 1.800 tačaka. Ovo hvata greške koje poznati primeri ne hvataju, jer ne zna šta je odgovor — zna samo šta mora da važi. Invariants on 200 random beams: sums of forces and moments vanish, and the numerical derivative of the moment matches the shear (dM/dx = V) at 1,800 points. This catches errors closed-form examples miss, because it does not know the answer — only what must hold.
  • Nezavisna integracija: Iy T preseka se poredi sa numeričkim integraljenjem po visini u 400.000 traka, a ne sa drugom formulom. Independent integration: the Iy of a T-section is compared against numerical integration over the depth in 400,000 strips, not against another formula.
  • Ravnoteža preseka u EN 1992: posle proračuna armature test proverava da je sila u betonu jednaka sili u armaturi i da njihov proizvod sa krakom vraća upravo zadati MEd. Rezultat se ne poredi sa zapamćenim brojem iz knjige nego sa fizikom. Section equilibrium in EN 1992: after the reinforcement calculation, the test verifies that the concrete force equals the steel force and that their product with the lever arm returns exactly the given MEd. The result is checked against physics, not against a number remembered from a book.
  • Granice: tri oslonca moraju da vrate odredjen: false sa objašnjenjem, a poziv unutrašnjih sila na takvom sistemu mora da baci grešku. Pretpostavljen Wpl mora da bude označen. Limits: three supports must return odredjen: false with an explanation, and asking for internal forces on such a system must throw. An assumed Wpl must be flagged.
  • Izmerena aproksimacija: I presek bez zaobljenja daje 95.0% tabličnog Iy za IPE 200; test zahteva da taj odnos ostane između 93% i 97% i da presek nosi napomenu. Measured approximation: the fillet-free I-section yields 95.0% of the tabulated Iy for IPE 200; the test requires that ratio to stay between 93% and 97% and the section to carry its note.

Zadaci ne računaju tačan odgovor sopstvenom formulom nego pozivaju isto jezgro. Ako se jezgro pokvari, pašće i test i zadatak — ne mogu da se raziđu u tišini. The task generator does not compute the correct answer with its own formula; it calls the same core. If the core breaks, both the test and the task break — they cannot drift apart silently.

Pošten sud o ovom pilotuHonest verdict on this pilot

Provereno: jezgro proračuna, sa 77 automatskih provera uključujući invarijante na nasumičnim gredama i ravnotežu preseka; materijalne tablice (EN 1992 tab. 3.1, EN 1993 tab. 3.1, EN 338, EN 1995 tab. 3.1) upisane su ručno iz standardne literature i proverene kroz te testove samo u pogledu doslednosti, ne prema overenom tekstu standarda. Verified: the calculation core, with 77 automatic checks including random-beam invariants and section equilibrium; the material tables (EN 1992 Tab. 3.1, EN 1993 Tab. 3.1, EN 338, EN 1995 Tab. 3.1) were entered by hand from standard literature and are checked by those tests only for internal consistency, not against a certified copy of the standard.

Zaključeno, ne izmereno: da program od 15 nedelja odgovara domaćem formatu — to je zaključak iz javnih stranica dva fakulteta, a ne iz zvaničnog silabusa predmeta koji prof. Darinka vodi. Pilot zato namerno ne tvrdi da je usklađen sa konkretnim akreditovanim predmetom: to je prvi razgovor koji sledi. Inferred, not measured: that the 15-week programme matches the local format — that is an inference from the public pages of two faculties, not from the official syllabus of the course Prof. Darinka teaches. The pilot therefore deliberately does not claim alignment with a specific accredited course: that is the first conversation to have.

Nije gledano: zvanični nacionalni prilozi (plaćen pristup preko ISS), tačne vrednosti αcc i ψ koeficijenata iz priloga za Srbiju, tablični Wpl i zaobljenja valjanih profila, i ponašanje sajta na čitaču ekrana. Ništa od toga nije prepreka za probni čas; sve je prepreka za tvrdnju „usklađeno sa standardom". Not examined: the official national annexes (paywalled via the national standards body), the exact αcc and ψ values in the Serbian annexes, tabulated Wpl and fillet geometry of rolled profiles, and screen-reader behaviour. None of this blocks a trial lecture; all of it blocks the claim "standard-compliant".
